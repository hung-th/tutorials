# Biology tutorials

Interactive tutorial pages for MBiol2 tutorials at St Hilda's College, Oxford.

**Website:** https://hung-th.github.io/tutorials/

| Tutorial | Topic | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is it really what's inside that counts? | Animal personality | [`animal-personality/`](animal-personality/) |
| What determines the spatial distribution of genetic diversity? | Population genetics | [`genetic-diversity/`](genetic-diversity/) |

Each tutorial is a single self-contained HTML file with no build step. Students need no preparation: the papers, data and interactive models are on the page.

## Case-study sources

- Ramadan, S. I., Hung, T. H., Delacoux, M., Kano, F., Inoue-Murayama, M. & Biro, D. (2026). *Physiology & Behavior*, 305, 115211. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physbeh.2025.115211
- Csilléry, K., Yang, H., Hung, T. H. et al. (2025). GenDivRange. *Scientific Data*, 12, 980. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-05303-2
- Kettle, C. & Boshier, D. (2014). Genetic bottlenecks in the restoration of *Araucaria nemorosa*. Forest Genetic Resources Training Guide, Bioversity International. Data from Kettle et al. (2007) *Molecular Ecology* and Kettle et al. (2008) *Biological Conservation*.
